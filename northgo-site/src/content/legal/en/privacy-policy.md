Northgo Inc. ("Northgo", "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect through northgo.com and how we use it.

## Data we collect

- **Enquiry and quote details** you submit, such as your name, company, email, phone number and shipment information.
- **Technical data** such as browser type, pages visited and approximate location, collected in aggregate to keep the site secure and improve it.

## How we use your data

We use your data to respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, provide logistics services, meet legal obligations and improve our website. We do not sell personal data.

## Sharing

We share data only where needed to deliver our services, for example with carriers, customs authorities and Northgo offices in other countries, and with service providers who host our website and email under confidentiality obligations.

## Retention and security

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law, and protect it with appropriate technical and organisational measures.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, or object to its processing. To make a request, email [info.tpe@northgo.com](mailto:info.tpe@northgo.com).
