This policy explains how northgo.com uses cookies and similar technologies.

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site work and remember your preferences.

## Cookies we use

- **Strictly necessary**: required for security and for features such as the enquiry form. These cannot be switched off.
- **Preferences**: remember choices such as your language.
- **Analytics** (only with your consent): help us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it.

We do not use cookies for advertising.

## Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop parts of the site from working.
