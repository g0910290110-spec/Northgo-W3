## Warehousing as part of the chain

Our warehouses are linked to our ocean, air and trucking services, so goods move from port to shelf to customer with fewer handovers and fewer delays.

## Value-added services

- Cross-docking and transloading
- Labelling, barcoding and repacking
- Quality inspection and photo reports
- Returns handling

## Secure and controlled

Access control, CCTV and clear handling procedures protect your goods, while regular reporting keeps your team informed.
