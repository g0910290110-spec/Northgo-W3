## From planning to placement

Project cargo leaves no room for improvisation. We start with a feasibility study covering dimensions, weights, lifting points and the full route, then build a schedule that aligns vessel, crane, truck and site readiness.

## What we coordinate

- Vessel and equipment chartering
- Cranes, rigging and lashing
- Escort vehicles and road permits
- Customs, duty exemptions and temporary import procedures
- Insurance and risk assessment

## One point of contact

A single project manager owns your shipment from kickoff to handover and keeps every partner, from shipping line to rigging crew, working to the same plan.
