## Planned around your cargo

Every shipment starts with a plan. Our specialists compare routes, carriers and transit times against your budget and deadline, then recommend the option that fits, with every charge listed upfront.

## Ocean freight

- **FCL**: 20', 40', high-cube, reefer and special equipment on schedules you can count on.
- **LCL**: weekly consolidations on main Asia and transpacific lanes, so you only pay for the space you use.
- **Origin and destination teams** that can optimise each shipment for cost, route or transit time.

## Air freight

When speed matters, we book capacity on direct and connecting services, handle dangerous goods documentation, and keep you updated at every milestone from booking to delivery.

## Land transport

Whether your cargo arrives by ship or plane, our trucking partners receive the information they need in advance, so containers and pallets move on without waiting.

## Customs clearance

Before importing a product, you must identify its Harmonized System (HS) code to calculate duties, tariffs and taxes. Our customs team acts as your second line of defence, validating classifications to prevent penalties and delays and make sure your goods arrive compliantly.
