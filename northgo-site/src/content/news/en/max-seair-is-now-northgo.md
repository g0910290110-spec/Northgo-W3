On 11 November 2024, Max Seair Logistics Inc., founded in Taipei in 1988, officially became **Northgo Inc.**

The new name reflects our forward-looking approach and our commitment to guiding clients toward new horizons in a fast-changing industry.

## What this means for our clients

- **Same team and contacts.** You will continue working with the people you already know.
- **Same services and agreements.** Existing contracts, rates and service levels remain in place.
- **New email domain.** Our addresses now end in `@northgo.com`.

Thank you for more than three decades of trust. We look forward to the next chapter with you.
